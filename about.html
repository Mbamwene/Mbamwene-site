Biography

Mbamwene Hlangano Mlotshwa is a versatile artist from Zambia, born on 21 August 2002. He is known for blending Afrosounds with rap and R&B, delivering songs that reflect his emotions and confidence. With a passion for music and fashion, Mbamwene has cultivated a lifestyle brand that integrates his music career, events through Kopavents, and the MHM2WENTY1NE clothing line.

His music journey has gained thousands of listeners across streaming platforms, with tracks like “D.I.M.E,” “Tornado,” and “AMWU (A Moment Wit' U)” showcasing his range and creativity.
